How the score works

01

Real text extraction

PDF pages and DOCX paragraphs are parsed locally with PDF.js and Mammoth. Scanned image-only PDFs contain no text — the analyzer will tell you if it finds none.

02

Skill matching

Both documents are scanned against 199 skills and their aliases (e.g. “JS” counts as JavaScript, “k8s” as Kubernetes, “SEO” and “Tally” count too). Score = matched job skills ÷ total job skills.

03

Semantic similarity

A TF-IDF cosine similarity between the two full texts measures how closely your resume's language mirrors the posting — beyond just keywords.
